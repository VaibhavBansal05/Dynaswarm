    ? Reference

    One seed, one run. The seed fixes the world and its sequence of breaks. Same seed, same everything.
    Breaks come in clusters. A neighbourhood goes dark, not a single road.
    Not every break matters. One away from the route costs nothing — the count moves, the route doesn't.
