    ? What every control does

    1 · Build the world

    preset
    Fills the four fields below from a known setup. Editing any field afterwards is fine — the preset is a starting point, not a lock.
    model
    How roads connect. Map-based gives every place a real position and joins nearby ones, which is why it can be drawn. Street grid is a regular lattice. Random joins places with no regard to distance and has no positions at all, so the page refuses to draw it rather than invent a layout.
    places
    How many junctions. More places means a longer route and a harder search.
    how connected
    How readily two places get a road. Higher means more roads and more alternatives when one breaks. Ignored by Street grid, whose connections are fixed by the lattice.
    seed
    The one number that decides everything random. The same seed always gives the same world and the same sequence of breaks — that is what makes a result reproducible. Change it and you get a different world and a different disruption.
    Generate
    Builds the world and immediately rebuilds the timeline for it.
    Re-roll seed
    Picks a random seed and generates again — for browsing variants of the same settings.

    2 · Design the disruption

    preset
    Sets one number only — breaks per step. Everything else stays where it is. That is genuinely all the four named scenarios differ by.
    breaks per step
    How often something goes wrong, on average. 0.3 means most steps are quiet — roughly three in four. Set it to 0 for a world that never breaks.
    roads per break
    How many roads a single break takes out at once. Breaks are clustered around one place, not scattered, so this reads as a whole neighbourhood going dark rather than one road closing.
    how long
    How many timesteps the run lasts.
    what kind of breaks
    The mix of what can happen: roads closing, reopening, getting slower or faster, being removed, or new ones appearing. The numbers are relative weights, not percentages.
    Build
    Rebuilds the timeline on the current world, leaving the world alone.

    Playback

    ⏮
    Back to the start.
    ⏪ ⏩
    Jump to the nearest step where something actually breaks. Most steps are quiet, so stepping one at a time is mostly waiting.
    ← →
    One step. Arrow keys do the same.
    play
    Runs it. Space bar toggles.
    slider
    Drag to any step.

    Reading the numbers

    best route
    The true cheapest route right now, recomputed exactly at every step. Watch it move — that is the problem this project exists to solve.
    broke this step
    A break that lands away from the route costs nothing, so this can be large while the best route does not budge. Disruption and relevant disruption are different things.
    edge ledger
    Roads are never really created or destroyed, only opened and closed, so the count must always balance. If it ever says DIVERGED, something is wrong and the page will say so rather than hide it.
